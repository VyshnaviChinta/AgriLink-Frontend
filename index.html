The Circular Solution

How AgriLink Works

A seamless digital bridge from crop harvesting to sustainable industrial energy.

01

1. Farmer Lists Stubble

Farmers register their crop residue (paddy, cotton, sugarcane) with estimated acreage via simple mobile access or toll-free call.

02

2. Mechanized Baling & Pickup

Our logistics network brings automated balers directly to the field, safely compacting and loading the straw without any farm effort.

03

3. Clean Industrial Conversion

Residue is processed into high-grade biomass pellets, bio-CNG, paper pulp, and sustainable power replacing fossil coal.
